# AppGallery listing: English (default language)

## App name

Live Race Stats

## Brief introduction

Your live race standing on the wrist: place, gaps and laps.

## Introduction

Live Race Stats shows your live standing during a race on your Huawei watch.
Enter the race ID and your bib number once, and the watch refreshes every minute
with what the timing site knows about you:

- overall and group place, for example 17/84
- gap to the rider ahead, the rider behind and the leader
- how those gaps changed on the last lap
- laps completed
- gap to and size of the neighbouring packs

Pick up to 9 fields on a round watch (10 on a rectangular one) and choose the
metric in each. If the signal drops, the last standing stays on screen until
the next update.

The app works with races timed on universalbicycle.team or a compatible site
you enter. The race must publish live stats, and the watch reaches the
internet through your paired phone.

Long-press the results to open the settings; swipe right to go back. The app
speaks English, Russian, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch,
Polish, Czech and Kazakh.
